# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in mcRPC a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Trolling or inflammatory remarks
- Publishing others’ private information
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers via GitHub Security advisories or the contact
listed in the repository profile. Maintainers will review and respond as
appropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.
